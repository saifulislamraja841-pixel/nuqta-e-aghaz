What is Nuqta-e-Aghaz

A point of beginning for the next generation.

Nuqta-e-Aghaz is an initiative of Islami Jamiat Talaba UET Lahore, built to welcome and empower university students. It brings students from campuses across Lahore together for talent recognition, leadership growth and creative entertainment.

Jamiat is a student organisation working for the well-rounded development of students: building leadership, sharpening communication, strengthening character and nurturing spiritual growth.
