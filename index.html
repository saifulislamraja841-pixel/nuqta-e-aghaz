What is Nuqta-e-Aghaz

A point of beginning for the next generation.

Nuqta-e-Aghaz is an initiative of Islami Jamiat Talaba UET Lahore, built to welcome and empower university students. It brings students from campuses across Lahore together for talent recognition, leadership growth and creative entertainment.

Jamiat is a student organisation working for the well-rounded development of students: building leadership, sharpening communication, strengthening character and nurturing spiritual growth.

FAQ

Good to know.

Who can attend Nuqta-e-Aghaz?

Students from any university or college can register. The main-event pass covers all segments and refreshments.

How much is the registration fee?

PKR 1,500 for the main event. Register before 20 November to get the 33% early-bird discount.

Do I need to register separately for competitions?

Yes. Competitions and the Talent Award have their own forms so judges can plan. Please also register for the main event to get your entry pass.

How do ambassador referrals get counted?

When someone opens the site through your link, your code is saved on their device for 30 days and filled into every form automatically. Friends can also type your code in the "Ambassador / Referral Code" field.

Where can I see updates after registering?

Open My Portal and sign in with your registered email and the last 4 digits of your phone number. All announcements for you appear there.
