Install on Android

  1. Opened this from WhatsApp? Tap at the top right and choose Open in Chrome.
  2. In Chrome, tap at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app (on some phones it says Add to Home screen), then Install.
  4. Open Nuqta from your apps, like any other app.
Or download the APK file

Install on iPhone

  1. Opened this from WhatsApp? Tap the Safari icon at the bottom right to open it in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. Open Nuqta from your home screen.
This only works from Safari (or Chrome on iOS 16.4 and newer). Apple doesn't allow installing apps from a downloaded file, which is why it works this way.

On a computer? Send this page to your phone and open it there.
